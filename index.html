Department of Tafsīr and Quranic Studies: A Tradition of Scholarly Rigor

Established as the nexus of classical Arabic philology and hermeneutical inquiry, the Department of Tafsīr and Quranic Studies fosters critical scholarship at the highest academic echelon.

In an era characterized by accelerated epistemological disruption and algorithmic information networks, the "Tafsir Symposium 2026" convenes global scholars, research fellows, and postgraduates to interrogate the continuities and dialectics within Islamic exegetical heritage.

Organized under the patronage of Ma'din Academy in academic synergy with SABCeR (Sayyid Ahmedul Bukhari Center for Research), this symposium serves as an international forum validating classical Usul while addressing pressing modern dilemmas across bioethics, digital humanities, and socio-cultural reform.

Core Thematic Tracks

Academic Tracks
Usul al-Tafsir & Classical Methodologies

Epistemological frameworks in foundational commentaries: al-Tabari, al-Zamakhshari, al-Razi, al-Qurtubi, and Ibn Kathir.

Thematic Hermeneutics (Tafsir Mawdu'i)

Synthesizing holistic Quranic worldviews on justice, governance, human rights, and environmental equilibrium.

Quranic Bioethics & Modern Challenges

Exegetical principles applied to genomic technologies, artificial intelligence ethics, and contemporary bio-jurisprudence.

Digital Tools & Computational Quranic Linguistics

Natural Language Processing (NLP), semantic graphs, and corpus analysis of classical exegesis.
